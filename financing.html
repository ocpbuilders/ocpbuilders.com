What to Expect in 2026

Terms, qualification and the lender's view.

Typical terms

  • Down payments typically 20 to 30 percent
  • Construction periods of 6 to 12 months
  • Many construction loans are interest-only during the build
  • Owner-occupied builds may qualify for lower down payments

Who usually qualifies

  • Strong credit and verifiable income
  • Cash reserves for down payment and costs
  • A clear plan, scope and budget
  • Land owned, under contract or identified
