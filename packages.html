The Base Rate

$180 per sq ft. What you get.

Pricing on this page covers the build phase only. Land, permits and site work are separate. Below is what the base specification includes.

Structure5/8-inch drywallBetter impact resistance and sound control between rooms and units.
EnvelopeEnergy-efficient windows and insulationLower utility costs and steadier temperatures year round.
Interior finishSmooth, modern wall texturesA clean finish that is easy to maintain and repaint.
FixturesMoen or Delta fixturesReliable, widely supported brands throughout.

What Changes the Price

The number moves with your choices.

We would rather you see the drivers now than find them in a change order later.

WindowsThick metal windows cost more than plastic. Size and glass type matter too.
Cabinetry and countertopsMaterial, thickness and custom work.
FlooringTile, hardwood and specialty finishes versus standard.
Fixtures and lightingUpgraded plumbing fixtures, lighting packages and hardware.
Roof and exteriorRoof type, stucco and exterior details.
Smart wiring and locksDigital locks, low-voltage wiring and automation.
Lot conditionsSlope, soil, access and utilities can change site costs.
Layout and sizeSquare footage, rooms and bathrooms set the base cost.
Scope and complexityCustom design, structural changes and added features.

Finish Packages

Three levels. You choose how far to take it.

Base

$180 per sq ft

Our standard specification, as listed above. A durable, clean build at the lowest price.

  • 5/8-inch drywall
  • Energy-efficient windows and insulation
  • Smooth modern textures
  • Moen or Delta fixtures

4,400 sq ft example: $792,000

Premium, Balanced Excellence

$190 per sq ft

Upgrades where they show most, without paying for every top-tier option.

  • Upgraded cabinetry and countertops
  • Upgraded flooring and fixtures
  • Enhanced lighting and hardware
  • Everything in Base

4,400 sq ft example: $836,000

Luxury, High-End

$200+ per sq ft

Top-tier materials and custom details. Priced by selection, so it depends on how much you want.

  • Premium materials throughout
  • Custom design details
  • Specialty windows, flooring and finishes
  • Everything in Premium

4,400 sq ft example: $880,000 and up

Examples multiply the rate by 4,400 sq ft for the build phase only. They are not quotes.
