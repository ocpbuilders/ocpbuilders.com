What It Is

A home inside of a home.

Every suite is its own space. There is no large common room, on purpose. Tenants are not meant to hang out together, and some never see each other. Light furnishings, high quality Schlage digital locks and a private bathroom in each suite.

Tenants like the privacy and the build quality. Owners like that eight to ten rooms mean eight to ten rent checks. These duplexes sit outside the city center and still close to the main working areas of Phoenix.

The live-in option

Live in one suite or one entire side and rent the rest. If you live on one side and rent four rooms at $270 per week, that is $1,080 per week, about $4,680 per month gross, toward your housing cost.

What Ownership Involves

Who does what.

Most owners lease through PadSplit. PadSplit takes on the tenant side. The property side still needs someone. Here is the split, so you know before you build.

PadSplit handles

  • Marketing and advertising the rooms
  • Screening applicants
  • Rent collection and weekly payments
  • Resident communication and disputes

The owner handles

  • Furnishing each room
  • Maintenance and repairs
  • Cleaning and room turns
  • Utilities, or a property manager who handles them

We can manage tenants for owners who want it. Most owners choose PadSplit and hire out the property work. PadSplit's fees and terms are set by PadSplit, so review them with PadSplit directly. Responsibilities above reflect PadSplit's published host guidance.

Why a duplex

In Phoenix, a duplex is reviewed through the residential permit track. Triplexes, fourplexes and apartment buildings require commercial permits, with commercial plan review, site plan approval and stricter fire requirements. That is why this model stays at two doors: fewer approvals, and a simpler path to a finished building.

Cost and funding

Base builds start at $180 per sq ft. At 4,400 sq ft, that is $792,000 for the build phase, as an example, not a quote. Land, permits, site work and furnishing are separate. Finish level moves the price.
