Residential

Homes and remodels.

For custom homes, remodels and renovations. Scope and finishes drive timing and price.

01

Discovery and scope meeting

We review your goals, your property or existing home, your budget and your timeline.

02

Design and permitting

Plans and permits, if applicable. For new homes we work with architects. For remodels we start with an on-site evaluation.

03

Budget and contract

You select a finish level and we confirm scope and price in writing. See packages and pricing.

04

Construction

Our team builds in-house with regular site updates and inspections.

05

Final walkthrough

We walk the finished home with you, correct any items and hand it over.

Inquire today

Commercial

Commercial builds.

For facility build-outs and commercial construction at any scale. Single sites and multi-location rollouts.

01

Scope call and site review

We confirm the use, size, location and schedule. For chains, we discuss rollout across locations.

02

Plans and commercial permitting

Commercial plan review applies, and new commercial projects in Phoenix generally need site plan approval first, if applicable. We coordinate this with you.

03

Construction

Built in-house on an agreed scope and schedule.

04

Inspection and handover

Required inspections are completed and the space is handed over ready to open.

Flagship

The signature duplex.

For owners who want a 4,400 sq ft duplex built as eight to ten private suites. Steps marked "if applicable" depend on what you already have.

01

Discovery and strategy meeting

We talk through your goals, budget, timeline and whether you want to live in it or own it hands-off.

02

Land acquisition and due diligence, if applicable

Already have land? We review it. Need land? We help you find and evaluate it before you buy.

03

Design and permitting, if applicable

Plans and permits for a duplex, which Phoenix reviews through the residential track.

04

Financing, if applicable

We introduce you to lenders we have worked with. See financing.

05

Construction

Six months or less once permits are approved.

06

Ready to begin

The building is done and the next stage starts. Furnish each suite, then list the rooms on PadSplit or have us manage tenants. Cleaning and maintenance sit with you or a property manager. You can also live in one suite. See how ownership works
